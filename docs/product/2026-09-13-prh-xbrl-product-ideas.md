# PRH XBRL: product ideas

PRH's digital financial-statement XBRL data is transformed into a clear,
versioned analytical model. The product value comes from removing the need
for users to work with XBRL codes, contexts, or taxonomies.

## 1. Industry and regional benchmarks

The product shows profitability, growth, leverage, and liquidity for a chosen
industry, region, and company-size segment. Users see distributions, peer-group
size, and coverage instead of a single average.

Potential customers include banks, investors, consultants, municipalities, and
companies assessing a market or customer base.

## 2. SME peer-benchmarking product

The product places a company in a fair peer group: the same industry, region,
size, and, when relevant, age. It shows whether the company's margins, growth,
solvency, or working capital differ meaningfully from relevant peers.

Potential customers include entrepreneurs, accounting firms, lenders, business
brokers, and M&A advisers.

## 3. Financial health and change monitoring

The product monitors transparent risk signals and material changes derived from
financial statements: weakening profitability, rising leverage, shrinking cash,
declining equity, and unusual statement structures. It does not claim to predict
bankruptcy without a validated model; it shows the underlying signals and facts.

Potential customers include lenders, insurers, procurement organisations,
landlords, and B2B companies monitoring customer or supplier risk.

## Shared product layer

All three products use the same foundation: company, reporting period,
normalised metric, value, source filing, and interpretation rule. Every shown
number must support drill-down to the original PRH filing and taxonomy reference.
